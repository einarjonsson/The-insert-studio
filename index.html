Appendix

How to print & assemble

Choose a tool

Pick any generator above. All of them run in your browser, with no account and no install.

Configure & preview

Set your notebook size (Regular or Passport) and the options for that tool. The live preview updates instantly.

Print on A4 landscape

Duplex printers get one PDF. Manual printers get a guided two-step flow: Side 1 first, then flip and print Side 2.

Fold, cut & enjoy

Regular: fold at 110mm, cut at 220mm and discard the waste strip. Passport: cut at the centre lines. Slip it into your TN.

Private by design. PDFs are generated on your device. Nothing you type is uploaded.
